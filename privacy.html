Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Eltomia is an AI-powered social media analytics product currently undergoing private technical testing.

Eltomia is not currently available as a public commercial service. This policy describes its limited proof-of-concept (POC) activities.

1. Information We Access

With authorization from the connected TikTok account, Eltomia may access information made available through approved TikTok APIs, including:

  • Basic account and profile information.
  • Account-level statistics, where available.
  • Public video information and performance metrics.

Actual data availability depends on TikTok's API permissions, capabilities, and applicable policies.

2. Purposes of Processing

Information may be used to:

  • Test TikTok account authorization and API connectivity.
  • Validate available social media metrics.
  • Evaluate analytics calculations and reporting.
  • Assess the feasibility of future AI-powered insights.

3. Storage and Security

The POC is designed to store necessary test data locally on a controlled development device. No cloud database is planned for this phase.

Access credentials and authentication tokens are handled separately from analytics data and must not be committed to public code repositories.

4. Data Sharing

Eltomia does not currently provide public accounts or commercial data-sharing features. Any future transfer of account data to third-party AI providers or processors will be assessed and disclosed before implementation.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored TikTok test data will be deleted within 30 days after completion of the POC. Data will be deleted earlier following withdrawal of authorization or a valid deletion request, where required by applicable platform rules.

Authentication credentials will be revoked or securely deleted when no longer needed. The deletion process will be implemented and verified before live data collection begins.

6. Your Choices

The connected account owner may withdraw TikTok authorization through the relevant TikTok account settings. Privacy and deletion requests can also be sent to the contact address below.

7. Contact

For questions about privacy or data deletion, please contact: eltomiasupport@gmail.com

8. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as the POC evolves. Material changes to data handling practices will be reflected in a revised version.
